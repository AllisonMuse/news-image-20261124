An entry for [make.fun's "Sum up 24 November's world news in one image" bounty](https://make.fun/bounty/news-image-20261124) ($100, 50 places paid). The program (`main.ts`, Node 22, standard
library only) copies `entry.png` to the output directory; the image is a
1024×1024 PNG drawn at build time by `draw_news.py` (Python + Pillow),
so both grader runs write identical bytes. No network is used at run time.

## v2 — 30 stories in 7 region groups

v1 (6 stories) missed the Round 3 top 50, so v2 keeps the 6 verified v1
stories and adds 24 more, all re-verified against the original reporting
for 24 November 2026. Claims are attributed in the image where the
reporting itself says they are unconfirmed. Nothing was invented.

### Middle East (6)
1. **Strait of Hormuz — Iran reports four tankers hit mines.** IRGC said
   it hit a container ship; the Joint Maritime Information Centre said the
   mine claims were unconfirmed.
2. **Riyadh — Houthi drone hits PIF tower; 11 injured.** Saudi Civil
   Defence: two Saudis, two Indians, three Nepalis, four Egyptians hurt.
3. **Yemen — Saudi airstrikes hit Hodeida telecom site; 17 dead.**
   Houthi claim; Saudi Arabia silent; the claims could not be
   independently verified.
4. **Gaza — Oxfam: Israeli attacks turn Gaza into 'toxic land'.** Report
   finds signs of contamination across nearly all of the territory.
5. **Iran — Singer lashed 74 times for singing unveiled.** Parastoo
   Ahmadi punished in Qom; three musicians and a producer also sentenced.
6. **UAE — Bus-truck crash in Ras al-Khaimah kills 11.**

### Global (4)
7. **Markets — Huang's return lifts Nvidia 32%, then AI rally cools.**
   Nasdaq +6.5% Monday on reinstatement; tech slips on Tuesday.
8. **Climate — COP31 ends without a climate pact.** First summit since
   2009 with no deal; fossil-fuel language blocked talks.
9. **Law — 14 countries back ICJ ruling on Rohingya genocide.** UK,
   Spain, Canada, Australia among those recognising the Myanmar ruling.
10. **UN — Guterres wants AI-risk framework within a year.** UN chief
    calls for a multilateral plan to manage AI risks.

### Europe (6)
11. **Ukraine war — Ukraine drones hit Russian ships.** Kyiv: cargo ship
    Sparta II struck in Gulf of Finland; ships hit off Libya.
12. **Ukraine — Russian drone kills 11 in Dymer, incl. girl, 11.** Rescue
    ends at a five-storey block in Kyiv region; a boy, 7, and a woman, 79,
    were pulled out alive.
13. **Ukraine — Night attacks on Zaporizhzhia kill 2, wound 30+.**
    Four minors and a baby among the wounded.
14. **France — Sarkozy and Bruni ordered to stand trial in 2027.**
    Judges order trial over alleged witness tampering in the Libyan
    funding case.
15. **Greece — Greece's oldest museum reopens on the Acropolis.** Old
    Acropolis Museum returns after a seven-year, €6.2m restoration.
16. **Belgium — Austerity strike cancels flights in Belgium.** Tens of
    thousands protest; King gives PM more time on budget.

### East Asia (3)
17. **South Korea — Russian jets enter air-defence zone.** Seoul scrambles
    fighters near Ulleungdo; no territorial airspace was violated.
    (Earlier reports misidentified the aircraft as possibly North Korean;
    the military corrected this.)
18. **China — Shanghai party chief Chen Jining under graft probe.**
    Politburo member investigated for 'serious violations of discipline
    and law'.
19. **China — Tiangong station welcomes first foreign astronaut.**
    Pakistan's Zeeshan Ali to join the Shenzhou-24 mission.

### Africa (1)
20. **Sudan — Drone strike hits UN building in Khartoum.** Army blames
    paramilitary RSF; says attack launched from Ethiopian territory.

### South & Central Asia (5)
21. **Nepal — Quake strikes Nepal near China border.** Magnitude 5.4;
    tremors felt across India, Bhutan and Bangladesh.
22. **Pakistan — 4.1 quake jolts Pakistan early Tuesday.** NCS: 7:46 am,
    17 km deep, epicentre in Balochistan.
23. **India & Nepal — India and Nepal sign 8 MoUs; ₹15,000-crore
    package.** New Delhi announces grants and credit line; leaders flag
    off express train.
24. **Bangladesh — Hasina to be jailed if she returns from India.**
    Dhaka seeks her extradition; she says she will return on 16 December.
25. **Tajikistan — Tajikistan formally begins ICC withdrawal.** Fourth
    country to quit the court since US urged exits in July.

### Americas (5)
26. **New York, USA — Huawei guilty on 10 of 12 counts.** US jury
    convicts on trade secrets and bank fraud; acquits on racketeering.
27. **USA — Trump halts Iowa detonation of 68 barrels of explosives.**
    Army told to wait for court approval; lead azide too unstable to move.
28. **USA — Rep. Jimmy Gomez to resign after ethics report.** Panel found
    'substantial evidence' of sexual misconduct toward a staffer.
29. **Mexico — Mexico to try 'El Chapo Isidro' despite US bid.** Fausto
    Meza Flores faces drug and weapons charges after capture in Sinaloa.
30. **Colombia — Car bomb destroys buildings in Villavicencio.** No
    confirmed victims, president says.
